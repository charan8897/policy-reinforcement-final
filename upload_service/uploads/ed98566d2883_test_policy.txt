COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT

Section 1: Leave Policy
All employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees must request leave at least 7 days in advance through the HR system. Emergency leave (with medical certificate) can be approved within 24 hours.

Section 2: Remote Work Policy
Employees in grades 4 and above are eligible for 2 days of remote work per week. Remote work must be pre-approved by the manager. Maximum 10 remote days per month.

Section 3: Expense Reimbursement
Daily meal allowance is $25 USD for local travel and $50 USD for international travel. All expenses must be documented with receipts. Reimbursement is processed within 30 days.

Section 4: Overtime Policy
Overtime is compensated at 1.5x the regular rate for up to 10 hours per week. Overtime exceeding 10 hours per week requires approval from department head. Maximum 50 hours per month overtime.
